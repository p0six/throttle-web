What Throttle does

  • Runs on your Mac

    Language models run on your own computer instead of someone else's servers.

  • Makes pictures, video, voice and music

    From a first prompt to a finished clip, in one place.

  • Publishes when you say so

    One Publish button sends a finished video or picture to your own Instagram or TikTok account. Nothing is ever posted by itself.

  • Yours alone

    Throttle is not a public service. There is nothing to sign up for, and only its owner can connect accounts or publish with it.

How publishing works

Throttle can send what you make to your own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It uses the platforms' official APIs, and it acts only when you press Publish.

  1. Connect your own account

    You sign in to TikTok or Instagram yourself. The sign-in is saved on your Mac, in a file only you can read.

  2. Press Publish

    Pick a video or picture you made, write a caption and choose where it goes.

  3. It goes to your account

    To TikTok as a draft in your inbox or as a post. To Instagram as a Reel, a post or a Story.

A personal tool, not a public service

Throttle runs on its owner's computer and has no servers of its own. This website is here so that anyone, including the platforms Throttle connects to, can read what it does and how it handles data.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Throttle is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for, and only its owner can connect accounts or publish with it.

In short

  • Everything Throttle handles stays on its owner's own computer.
  • Throttle has no servers of its own. Its author receives nothing.
  • It talks to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube only to sign in, to publish and to check on a post.
  • Pressing Disconnect deletes the saved sign-in at once.

What Throttle receives

When the owner connects their own TikTok or Instagram account or their own YouTube channel, Throttle receives from that platform:

  • The account's basic profile: its name, username, profile picture and account id. Throttle uses it only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens. Throttle uses them only so that the owner can publish to their own account.
  • What the account may post, such as who can be allowed to see a post. Throttle shows these choices when the owner publishes.
  • From YouTube, how a video that Throttle uploaded was saved: who can see it and whether YouTube could process it. Throttle shows that on the post.

Throttle does not read followers, messages, comments or anybody else's content.

What Throttle sends

When the owner presses Publish, Throttle sends the chosen video or picture, its caption and the settings chosen for the post to the platform the owner picked. Afterwards it asks that platform how the post is doing and where it can be seen.

Nothing is published by itself. Every post is started by the owner.

YouTube

Throttle uses YouTube API Services to upload to YouTube and to check on what it uploaded. What YouTube and Google receive is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Where it is kept

All of it stays on the owner's own computer:

  • the sign-ins, in a file only the owner can read;
  • a history of what was published, with its caption, the time and the link to the post.

Throttle has no servers of its own. It talks only to TikTok, to Instagram (Meta) and to YouTube (Google) themselves. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising, for analytics or to train AI models.

How long it is kept

A sign-in is kept until the owner disconnects the account or the platform ends it. The history is kept until the owner deletes it.

Removing it

  • Pressing Disconnect in Throttle deletes the saved sign-in from the computer at once.
  • Access can also be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok or Instagram app, in the settings that list the apps connected to the account. For YouTube that is Google's page for apps with access to your account.
  • Because everything is on the owner's own computer, there is no copy anywhere else to delete.
  • A post that is already published stays on the platform until it is deleted there.

This website

This website is a few plain pages. It sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of a visit, such as the IP address. GitHub's privacy statement explains what it does with them.

Children

Throttle is not offered to the public and is not directed at children.
